### Week 1 – Initiation and literature exploration  
**Date:** 27/01/2026 (Zoom)  
**Attendance:** All five members  

**Objective**  
To initiate the project and establish a foundational understanding of jewellery image retrieval through literature review.

**Tasks**  
- Reviewed research in jewellery, fashion, and general image retrieval  
- Analysed model architectures (e.g., CNN-based approaches such as ResNet)  
- Examined feature extraction methods and similarity measures  

**Discussion**  
Members presented findings and compared approaches across studies. The discussion focused on the effectiveness of CNN-based feature extraction and the role of similarity metrics in retrieval systems.

**Decisions**  
- CNN models were selected as the initial baseline for feature extraction  
- A comparative direction was defined: CNN vs transformer-based methods (OpenCLIP)  
- The project was framed as a **retrieval-first system with similarity-based evaluation**
