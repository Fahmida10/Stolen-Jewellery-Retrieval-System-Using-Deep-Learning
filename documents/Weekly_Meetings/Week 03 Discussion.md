### Week 3 – Model selection (CNN baselines)  
**Date:** 10/02/2026 (Zoom)  
**Attendance:** All five members  

**Objective**  
To select appropriate baseline models for feature extraction and retrieval comparison.

**Tasks**  
- Reviewed candidate CNN architectures  
- Evaluated suitability for feature extraction in retrieval tasks  
- Selected baseline models:
  - ResNet18  
  - VGG16  
  - EfficientNet-B0  

**Discussion**  
The group emphasised the need for strong and widely accepted baselines prior to introducing advanced models. CNN architectures were considered reliable for feature extraction and suitable for benchmarking retrieval performance.

**Decisions**  
- Three CNN baselines (ResNet18, VGG16, EfficientNet-B0) were finalised  
- These models will serve as **reference points for comparison** with transformer-based approaches  
- A comparative study design (CNN vs OpenCLIP) was confirmed
